---
name: delete-freemium-account
description: Use when the customer wants to delete a freemium account and the reply should ask for the specific platform to provide correct steps.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is unsure about how to delete a freemium account.
2. Explain that the deletion process can differ depending on the platform or service.
3. Request the customer to specify which platform or service the freemium account is on.
4. Assure the customer that you will guide them through the appropriate deletion steps once the platform is known.
