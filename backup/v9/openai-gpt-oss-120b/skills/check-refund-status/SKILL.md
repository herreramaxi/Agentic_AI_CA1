---
name: check-refund-status
description: Use when the customer wants to see the refund status and the reply should request order details to locate the refund.
---

1. Ask the customer for additional information such as the order number, the date of the refund request, or any other details that could help locate the refund in the system.
2. Once the information is received, provide the most accurate and up-to-date refund status.
