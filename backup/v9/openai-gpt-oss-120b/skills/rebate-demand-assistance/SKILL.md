---
name: rebate-demand-assistance
description: Use when the customer wants help demanding a rebate and the reply should request purchase details and supporting documentation.
---

1. Acknowledge the customer's need for assistance with demanding a rebate.
2. Ask the customer to provide details about the rebate request, including:
   - The product or service purchased.
   - The date of purchase.
   - Any supporting documentation they have.
3. Explain that with this information you can help formulate a strong demand and improve their chances of receiving the rebate.
