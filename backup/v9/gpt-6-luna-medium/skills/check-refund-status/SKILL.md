---
name: check-refund-status
description: Use when a customer asks for help checking the status of a refund and the reply should request details to locate it.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking assistance with checking the status of their refund.
2. Ask them to provide additional information, such as the order number, date of the refund request, or any other details that could help locate the refund in the system.
3. Explain that once you have the information, you can provide the most accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Thank them for reaching out and appreciate their patience as you work together to resolve the matter.
