---

1. Acknowledge the customer's need for refund assistance and recognize that the situation can be frustrating.
2. Reassure the customer that you are there to provide guidance and support.
3. Recommend contacting the customer support team to initiate the refund request. Provide the phone number {{Customer Support Phone Number}} or Live Chat at {{Website URL}}.
4. Ask the customer to explain their situation in detail to the support team so they can guide them through the necessary steps and process the refund efficiently.
5. Reassure the customer of the commitment to their satisfaction and to addressing their concerns promptly and effectively.
