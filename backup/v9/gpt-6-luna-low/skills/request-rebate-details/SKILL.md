---
name: request-rebate-details
description: Use when the customer needs help demanding a rebate and the reply should ask for purchase details and supporting documentation.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer needs assistance demanding a rebate.
2. Explain that rebates can be a complex process and offer to guide them toward a favorable resolution.
3. Ask them to provide the product or service purchased, the date of purchase, and any supporting documentation.
4. Explain that this information will help formulate a strong demand and improve their chances of receiving the rebate.
