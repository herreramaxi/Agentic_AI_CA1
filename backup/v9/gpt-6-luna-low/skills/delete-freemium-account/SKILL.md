---
name: delete-freemium-account
description: Use when a customer asks how to delete a freemium account and the reply should request the platform or service they use.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is unsure how to delete a freemium account and offer assistance.
2. Explain that the deletion process can vary by platform or service.
3. Ask the customer to specify the platform or service where they have the freemium account so you can provide accurate steps.
4. Reassure the customer that you are there to help make the process smooth.
