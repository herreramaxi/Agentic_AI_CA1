name: open-account
description: Use when a customer wants to open a {{Account Category}} account and the reply should request the details needed to begin.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in opening a {{Account Category}} account and offer assistance.
2. Ask for their full name, email address, and preferred username to associate with the account.
3. Explain that after receiving these details, you will guide them through the process and inform them about the account’s benefits and features.
4. Reassure them that their privacy and security are priorities and that shared information will be handled with care and protection.
5. Welcome them to get started with accessing the benefits of a {{Account Category}} account.
