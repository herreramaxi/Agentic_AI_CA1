---
name: compensation-request
description: Use when a customer seeks compensation and the reply should ask for details about the issue before guiding them through the request.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking compensation and offer assistance.
2. Ask them to provide more details about what happened so you can guide them through the appropriate steps to request compensation.
3. Reassure them that you’ll do your best to reach a satisfactory resolution and ensure they feel valued.
