---
name: earn-products-opportunities
description: Use when a customer asks about earning products and the reply should request details to identify suitable programs or promotions.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in earning products.
2. Explain that the primary focus is selling items, but various programs and promotions may provide opportunities to earn products.
3. Ask which specific product or program they are interested in so you can assist and guide them toward suitable options.
