description: Use when a customer asks for information about terminating their account and the reply should request account details or specific questions.
---

1. Acknowledge the inquiry about terminating the customer’s {{Account Type}} account.
2. Recognize that they want information about the process and steps involved, and express willingness to help.
3. Ask them to provide their account details or any specific concerns or questions about the termination process.
4. Reassure them that you are available to help ensure a smooth experience.
