---
name: check-refund-status
description: Use when a customer wants help checking a refund status and the reply should request details to locate the refund.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking help checking the status of their refund and say we're here to help.
2. Ask for additional information, such as the order number, date of the refund request, or other details that could help locate the refund in the system.
3. Explain that once the information is provided, we can give the most accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Thank the customer for reaching out and express appreciation for their patience as we work together to resolve the matter.
