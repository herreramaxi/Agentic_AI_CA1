name: account-termination-information
description: Use when the customer asks for information about terminating their {{Account Type}} account and the reply should acknowledge the request and ask for account details or specific concerns.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer's message about the termination of their {{Account Type}} account has been received.
2. State that you understand they would like to gather information regarding the process and steps involved in terminating the account.
3. Express that you value their decision and want to assist them in the best possible way.
4. Ask the customer to provide their account details or any specific concerns or questions they have regarding the termination process, so accurate information can be given.
5. Close by saying you are there to help and want to ensure they have a smooth and hassle-free experience.
