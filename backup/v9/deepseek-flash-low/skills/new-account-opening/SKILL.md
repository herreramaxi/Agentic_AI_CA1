## Steps

1. Thank the customer for their interest in opening a {{Account Category}} account with us, and let them know you will assist them every step of the way.
2. Ask for their full name, email address, and a preferred username to associate with the account.
3. Once these details are received, guide them through the process.
4. Make sure they are well-informed about the benefits and features of the {{Account Category}} account.
5. Reassure them that their privacy and security are top priorities and that any information they share will be handled with the utmost care and protection.
