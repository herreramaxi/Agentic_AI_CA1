# Compensation Request Clarification

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking compensation and show willingness to help with the matter.
2. Ask the customer to provide more details about what happened, so their situation is understood.
3. Explain that these details will allow you to guide them through the appropriate steps to request compensation effectively.
4. Reassure the customer that you will do your best to ensure a satisfactory resolution and that they feel valued throughout the process.
