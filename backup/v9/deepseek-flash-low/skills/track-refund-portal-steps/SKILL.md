## Steps

1. Acknowledge that the customer is unsure how to track their restitution, and note that tracking a refund matters for transparency and peace of mind.
2. Offer to guide them through the process, then give these steps:
   1. Visit our website and log in to your account.
   2. Navigate to the 'My Orders' or 'My Account' section.
   3. Look for the 'Refunds' or 'Returns' tab and click on it.
   4. You should see a list of your recent refund requests. Locate the refund you are interested in tracking.
   5. Click on the corresponding refund request to access the detailed information.
   6. You will find the refund status, including whether it is pending, approved, or processed. You may also find additional details such as the date of the refund initiation and the expected refund amount.
   7. If you have any further questions or need assistance with tracking your restitution, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
3. Close by hoping the steps help them track their refund successfully, and ask if there is anything else you can assist them with.
