## Steps

1. Thank the customer for their interest in earning some of our products.
2. Explain that our primary focus is on selling our items, but that we do have various programs and promotions that can provide opportunities for customers to earn products.
3. Ask the customer to provide more details on the specific product or program they are interested in.
4. Explain that this will allow you to assist them better and guide them towards the most suitable options for earning our products.
