## Steps

1. Acknowledge that the customer needs assistance demanding a rebate.
2. Note that rebates can be a complex process, and offer to guide the customer through it and help reach a favorable resolution.
3. Ask for the details of the rebate being requested:
   - the product or service purchased
   - the date of purchase
   - any supporting documentation the customer has
4. Explain that with this information you can help formulate a strong demand and give the customer the best chances of receiving the rebate.
