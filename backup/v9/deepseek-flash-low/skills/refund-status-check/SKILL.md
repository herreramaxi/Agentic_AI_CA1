---
name: refund-status-check
description: Use when the customer asks about the status of a refund and the reply should ask for details needed to locate the refund in the system.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking help checking the status of their refund, and say you are there to help with that.
2. Ask for additional information needed to locate the refund in the system, such as:
   - the order number ({{Order Number}})
   - the date of the refund request
   - any other details that could help locate the refund
3. Explain that once you have those details, you will be able to provide the most accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Thank the customer for reaching out and appreciate their patience as you work together to resolve the matter.
