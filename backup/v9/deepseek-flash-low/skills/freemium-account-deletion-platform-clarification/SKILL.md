---
name: freemium-account-deletion-platform-clarification
description: Use when the customer wants to delete a freemium account but is unsure how, and the reply should acknowledge their uncertainty and ask which platform or service the account is with.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is unsure about the process of deleting a freemium account and offer assistance.
2. Explain that deleting the account can vary depending on the specific platform or service being used.
3. Ask the customer to specify the platform or service their freemium account is with.
4. Explain that this information will allow you to guide them through the correct deletion process.
5. Reassure the customer that you are there to help make the process as smooth as possible.
