## Steps

1. Thank the customer for contacting for assistance with ordering articles.
2. Say you are here to provide the guidance needed to make a seamless purchase.
3. Ask the customer to provide the specific details of the articles they are interested in.
4. Explain that this will allow you to assist more effectively and ensure a smooth ordering process.
