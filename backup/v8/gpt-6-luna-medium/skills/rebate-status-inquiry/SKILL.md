---
name: rebate-status-inquiry
description: Use when a customer cannot check for updates on a rebate and the reply should ask for information to look up its status.
---

1. Acknowledge the customer's frustration about not being able to check for rebate updates.
2. Offer to assist with the concern.
3. Ask the customer to provide their order number or any relevant information so you can look into the rebate status.
4. Reassure the customer that you will work together to find a solution and keep them informed.
