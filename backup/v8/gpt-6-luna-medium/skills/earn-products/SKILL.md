description: Use when a customer asks about earning products and the reply should explain possible programs and ask what product or program they mean.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in earning products.
2. Explain that the primary focus is selling items, but various programs and promotions may offer opportunities to earn products.
3. Ask for details about the specific product or program they are interested in, so you can guide them toward suitable options.
