---
name: create-account
description: "Use when a customer wants help creating an account and the reply should request the prospective account holder's details before guiding them through setup."
---

1. Enthusiastically offer to assist with creating a {{Account Category}} account for the customer’s intended account holder.
2. Explain that getting started requires the account holder’s full name, email address, and preferred username.
3. Say that once these details are provided, you will guide them through the process step by step and keep them informed.
4. Reassure them that customer security and privacy are prioritized and shared data will be handled with care.
5. Ask them to provide the requested information and invite them to ask questions along the way.
