---
name: track-rebate
description: Use when a customer asks how to track their rebate and the reply should acknowledge the uncertainty and request rebate details.
---

Acknowledge the customer's uncertainty about tracking their rebate and explain that tracking is an important step. Offer to guide them through the process. Ask them to provide details of their rebate, such as the purchase date, the rebate offer they used, or any documentation they received, so you can provide accurate tracking instructions.
