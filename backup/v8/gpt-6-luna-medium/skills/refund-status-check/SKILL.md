---
name: refund-status-check
description: Use when a customer asks whether something is wrong with their refund and the reply should request details to investigate and provide an update.
---

1. Acknowledge that it is important for the customer to know their refund is on track and reassure them that you can help resolve any concerns.
2. Ask for their refund reference number or other relevant details so you can investigate and provide an accurate update.
3. Thank them for their cooperation and say you look forward to assisting them.
