1. When you need details to investigate a customer’s issue, acknowledge their goal, ask what happened, and reassure them you’ll help find an appropriate resolution - this keeps the reply supportive without adding unsupported requirements.
2. For clear how-to requests, answer directly with concise guidance rather than asking for clarification first - this lets customers make progress immediately.
3. For requests to check the status of a specific transaction or refund, acknowledge the request and ask for identifying details needed to locate it; reassure them you’ll help provide an accurate update - this avoids unsupported self-service directions when lookup is required.
4. When customers mention financial difficulty, respond with empathy and avoid implying that the company caused it - this acknowledges their circumstances without assigning blame.
5. For routine account-opening questions, warmly acknowledge the customer’s interest and ask for the basic details needed to get started; reassure them you’ll guide them through the process and handle their information carefully - this moves the conversation forward and feels welcoming.
6. For routine cancellation or account-removal requests, acknowledge the customer's decision and ask for the account identification details needed to proceed; keep the tone courteous and reassure them you can assist - this moves the request forward without diverting them to unspecified concerns.
7. When customers express interest in earning or acquiring products, warmly acknowledge their interest and ask which product or program they mean and what opportunity they have in mind - this enables tailored guidance without assuming a specific route.

For requests to receive a rebate, reimbursement, or refund, acknowledge the goal, apologize for any inconvenience, and direct the customer to customer support through available contact channels; use a warm, reassuring tone without asking unnecessary clarifying questions - this provides a clear next step for a request handled by support.
9. When a how-to request depends on which platform or service the customer uses, acknowledge their goal and ask which platform or service they mean before giving steps; reassure them you can guide them once clarified - this avoids assuming a process.
10. When a customer asks for help taking an action on a purchase, acknowledge their request warmly and invite them to share what they want to buy or what guidance they need; reassure them you can help with the ordering process - this opens the conversation without unsupported procedural directions.
