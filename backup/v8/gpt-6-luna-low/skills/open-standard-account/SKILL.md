---
name: open-standard-account
description: Use when an unregistered customer asks how to open a standard account and the reply should explain the registration process.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in opening a {{Account Type}} account and acknowledge that they haven't registered yet.
2. Guide them to visit the website and navigate to the account registration page.
3. Explain that they can fill out the user-friendly form with their personal information.
4. Tell them they will receive a confirmation email with further instructions to complete registration.
5. Let them know customer support can assist if they encounter difficulties or have questions, and invite them to ask for further help.
