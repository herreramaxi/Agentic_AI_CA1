---
name: product-earning-opportunities
description: Use when a customer asks about earning products and the reply should request details to guide them to relevant programs or promotions.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in earning products.
2. Explain that the primary focus is on selling products, but various programs and promotions may provide opportunities to earn products.
3. Ask which specific product or program they are interested in so you can guide them toward suitable options.
