1. Thank the customer for raising the issue with removing their {{Account Type}} account and apologize for the difficulty.
2. Reassure them that you are there to help resolve the matter promptly.
3. Ask for more details about the specific issue they are encountering so you can understand it and provide an appropriate solution.
4. Explain that their feedback will help identify and address obstacles in the account removal process.
