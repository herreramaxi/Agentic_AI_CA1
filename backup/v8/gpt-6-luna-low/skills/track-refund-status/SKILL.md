---
name: track-refund-status
description: Use when a customer wants to track a restitution or refund and the reply should explain where to find its status online.
---

1. Visit our website and log in to your account.
2. Navigate to the 'My Orders' or 'My Account' section.
3. Look for the 'Refunds' or 'Returns' tab and click on it.
4. Locate the refund you are interested in tracking in the list of recent refund requests.
5. Click on the refund request to access the detailed information.
6. Check the refund status, such as whether it is pending, approved, or processed. Review any additional details, including the date of the refund initiation and the expected refund amount.
7. If you have further questions or need assistance with tracking your restitution, reach out to our customer support team.
