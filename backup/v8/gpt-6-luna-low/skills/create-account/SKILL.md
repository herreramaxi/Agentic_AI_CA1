---
name: create-account
description: Use when a customer wants to create an account for someone else and the reply should request the required details and offer step-by-step help.
---

1. Offer to assist in creating a {{Account Category}} account for the person.
2. Ask for their full name, email address, and preferred username.
3. Explain that you'll guide them through the process step by step.
4. Reassure them that customer data is handled securely and privately.
5. Invite them to provide the requested information and ask any questions.
