---
name: compensation-request
description: Use when a customer seeks compensation and the reply should ask for details about the issue before guiding them through the request.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking compensation for an issue and offer assistance.
2. Ask the customer to provide more details about what happened so you can guide them through the appropriate steps to request compensation.
3. Reassure the customer that you will do your best to reach a satisfactory resolution and ensure they feel valued.
