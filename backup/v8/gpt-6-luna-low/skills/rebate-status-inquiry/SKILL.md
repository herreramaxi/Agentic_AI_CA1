---
name: rebate-status-inquiry
description: Use when the customer cannot check for rebate updates and the reply should request identifying information to look up the status.
---

Acknowledge the customer's frustration about being unable to check rebate updates.
Offer to help with the concern.
Ask the customer to provide their order number or any relevant information so you can look into the rebate status.
Reassure the customer that you will work with them to find a solution and keep them informed.
