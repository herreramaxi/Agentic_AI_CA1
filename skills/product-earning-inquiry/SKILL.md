---
name: product-earning-inquiry
description: Use when a customer asks how to earn products and the reply should explain that programs or promotions may offer opportunities and ask for details.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in earning products.
2. Explain that the primary focus is selling items, but various programs and promotions may provide opportunities for customers to earn products.
3. Ask the customer for more details about the specific product or program they are interested in so you can assist them and guide them toward suitable options.
