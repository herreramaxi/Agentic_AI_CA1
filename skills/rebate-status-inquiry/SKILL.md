---
name: rebate-status-inquiry
description: Use when the customer cannot check for rebate updates and the reply should ask for information to look up the rebate status.
---

1. Acknowledge the customer's frustration about being unable to check for rebate updates.
2. Offer assistance with the concern.
3. Ask the customer to provide their order number or other relevant information so you can look up the rebate status.
4. Reassure the customer that you will find a solution and keep them informed.
