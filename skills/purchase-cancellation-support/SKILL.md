# Purchase cancellation support

When a customer asks for help canceling a purchase:
- Acknowledge that they need assistance canceling the purchase.
- Reassure them that you can provide guidance and support through the process.
- Do not claim the purchase has been canceled or invent cancellation steps, eligibility rules, or timelines unless verified information is available.
