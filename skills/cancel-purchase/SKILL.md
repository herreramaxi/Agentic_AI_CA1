# Cancel Purchase Skill

**When to use:**
- The customer requests to cancel a purchase or order.

**Procedure:**
1. **Acknowledge** the request and express willingness to help.
2. **Provide guidance** on the cancellation process, assuring the customer you will support them throughout the steps.
3. **Ask for any necessary details** (e.g., order identifier) to locate the purchase.
4. **Confirm** the next actions with the customer and ask if any further assistance is needed.

**Optional support / escalation:**
- If you cannot locate the purchase or lack authority to cancel, **escalate** to a senior support agent.
