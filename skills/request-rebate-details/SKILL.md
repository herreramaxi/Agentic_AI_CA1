---
name: request-rebate-details
description: Use when the customer needs help demanding a rebate and the reply should request the purchase and supporting information.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer needs assistance demanding a rebate and offer guidance.
2. Ask for the product or service purchased, the date of purchase, and any supporting documentation.
3. Explain that these details will help formulate a strong demand and improve the chances of receiving the rebate.
