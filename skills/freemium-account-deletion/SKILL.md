---
name: freemium-account-deletion
description: Use when a customer wants to delete a freemium account and the reply should ask which platform or service it is with.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is unsure how to delete their freemium account and offer assistance.
2. Explain that account deletion steps can vary depending on the platform or service.
3. Ask the customer to specify which platform or service their freemium account is with so you can provide accurate steps.
4. Reassure the customer that you are there to help make the process smooth.
