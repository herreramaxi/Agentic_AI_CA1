---
name: open-standard-account
description: Use when an unregistered customer asks how to open a standard account and the reply should explain the registration steps.
---

1. Thank the customer for expressing interest in opening a {{Account Type}} account and acknowledge that they haven't registered yet.
2. Explain that they can visit the website and navigate to the account registration page.
3. Tell them to complete the user-friendly form with their personal information.
4. Explain that after providing the necessary details, they will receive a confirmation email with further instructions to complete registration.
5. Offer customer support if they encounter difficulties or have questions, and invite them to ask if they need further help.
