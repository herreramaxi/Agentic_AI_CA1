---
name: reimbursement-details-request
description: Use when a customer seeks reimbursement and the reply should ask for the transaction or purchase details needed to assist.
---

1. Acknowledge that the customer is seeking a reimbursement and apologize for any inconvenience or frustration.
2. Ask the customer to provide more information about the reimbursement request, specifically the transaction or purchase they are seeking reimbursement for.
3. Explain that the information will help expedite the process and address the concern accurately.
4. Reassure the customer that you are available to guide them through the necessary steps.
