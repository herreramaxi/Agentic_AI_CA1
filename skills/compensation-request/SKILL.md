---
name: compensation-request
description: Use when the customer asks for compensation and the reply should request details about the issue before advising on next steps.
---

Acknowledge that the customer is seeking compensation for a particular issue and offer assistance.
Ask the customer to provide more details about what happened so you can guide them through the appropriate steps to request compensation.
Reassure them that you will do your best to reach a satisfactory resolution and help them feel valued throughout the process.
