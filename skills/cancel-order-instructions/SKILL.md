## Canceling an order

Guide the customer through the standard self-service cancellation flow:
1. Sign in to the account through {{Online Company Portal Info}}.
2. Open the orders area, labeled '{{Online Order Interaction}}' (the reference may use the same placeholder for an alternate section name).
3. Find {{Order Number}} and open its details.
4. Select the cancellation option labeled '{{Online Order Interaction}}'.
5. If prompted, confirm the cancellation and follow the on-screen instructions to finish.

If the customer has difficulty or further questions, direct them to the team during {{Customer Support Hours}} at {{Customer Support Phone Number}}, or to Live Chat via {{Website URL}}.
