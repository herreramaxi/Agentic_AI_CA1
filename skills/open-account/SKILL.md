---
name: open-account
description: Use when a customer asks for help opening an account and the reply should request their details to begin.
---

1. Thank the customer for their interest in opening a {{Account Category}} account and offer assistance.
2. Ask for their full name, email address, and preferred username.
3. Explain that once you have these details, you will guide them through the process and inform them about the account’s benefits and features.
4. Reassure them that their privacy and security are top priorities and that shared information will be handled with care and protection.
